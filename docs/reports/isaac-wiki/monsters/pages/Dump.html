Dump is a monster that is exclusive to Dross.

Dump is consistently in a puddle of Brown Creep and leaves a trail while chasing Isaac. When it takes enough damage, its head comes off and flies around the room while its body spews out brown shots like a geyser until it empties and disappears into the ground.

This enemy has 3 different facial expressions, all of which are purely cosmetic and don't affect how its head looks after it detaches.

Dump Head refers to the head of Dump once its body takes enough damage, although it can also spawn naturally in rooms.

It slides towards Isaac and leaves a trail of slippery brown creep. If split as a result of depleting the Dump’s health, it bounces off walls then chases Isaac.
