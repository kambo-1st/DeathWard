Found on the Ascent to Home.

Tainted Pooters are corrupt and much tankier versions of Pooters. They move the same as Pooters, slowly drifting towards Isaac, however instead of firing just one blood shot at him, they shoot 4 big shots that become 2 smaller ones when in contact with a wall or obstacle.

They are exclusive to the Ascent to Home.

Bugs

Bug! If a Pooter or a Super Pooter is stunned at the same time that it tries to shoot Isaac after the effect wears off, their shot will be fired without its corresponding animation.
Bug! If a Pooter or a Super Pooter is stunned or feared at the same time that it tries to shoot Isaac, its sound effect will repeat continuously until the effect is gone.
