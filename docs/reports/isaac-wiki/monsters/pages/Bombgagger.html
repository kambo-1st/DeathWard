Bombgaggers are monsters added in The Binding of Isaac: Repentance DLC and are exclusively found in Ashpit.

When near Isaac, they will begin to vomit up a Giga Bomb and proceed to run to a safe distance. Will spawn a Giga Bomb if killed during the vomiting animation.

Notes

  • Safety Scissors can be used to defuse and pick up the Giga Bombs that Bombgaggers produce.

Trivia

  • Referred to as Toothman by the development team before Repentance's release.
