Fred is a monster that can be encountered in Chapter 4.

Fred fires an explosive Ipecac shot at Isaac. Fred can also jump out of the ground and towards Isaac and then dive back in, shooting four blood shots in the cardinal directions when it does so. It usually does this after firing an Ipecac shot or if Isaac is far away.

Trivia

  • In the original game, the shots that Freds fired when they dive back into the ground traveled diagonally instead of in the cardinal directions.
  • Freds appear to be babies with the medical condition harlequin ichthyosis.
