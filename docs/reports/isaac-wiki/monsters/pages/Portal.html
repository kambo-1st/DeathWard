Portals are enigmatic entities that appear on many floors/ only in The Void. They continuously spawn enemies until they are destroyed or spawn a certain number of enemies.

Portals spawn different enemies depending on the floor they appear on.

In The Void, Portals spawn enemies based on the theme of the room they are in.

Lil Portal (Flies Only)

There is a variant of the Lil Portal with the ID 306.1.1 that looks and behaves identically to regular Lil Portals but will only spawn Fly related monsters from its regular pool.

Entrance Portal

A special version of the Portal (dubbed the "Entrance Portal" to avoid confusion) serves as the entrance to The Void. It is found in ???, but can also be found after defeating Mom (5% chance and only if Isaac has 10+ tear damage or 20+ items), Mom's Heart/ It Lives (10% chance), Isaac/ Satan (15% chance), ???/ The Lamb (20% chance), or Mega Satan (50% chance). The Entrance Portal room in ??? gets darker the closer the player is to the portal.

Unlockable Achievements

  • Black Hole - Defeat 20 Portals.

Notes

  • Charming a Portal with Mom's Eyeshadow or similar items will cause the Portal to spawn permanently charmed enemies similar to the Friendly Ball.
    • As a result, a permanently charmed Death's Head can spawn from charmed Portals on Chapter 3 and remain permanently invulnerable. This is the only known method of charming an invulnerable enemy.
  • If a portal is spawned in ???, Chest, Dark Room or Home via Debug Console, the Portal will despawn a few seconds before spawning any monster.
  • If Portals aren't unlocked, certain enemies will spawn in their rooms instead, depending on the floor the room is in. For instance, Frowning Gapers spawn as Portal placeholders on Chapter 1 floors, whereas Vises spawn as Portal placeholders on Chapter 4 floors.
  • It is often wise to instantly run into portals with orbitals after entering the room, if possible. Catching the small window timeframe for this almost always results in their instant death without any consequences.

Trivia

  • Before v1.06.0185, portals continually spawned enemies until they were defeated.
  • Portals were able to spawn Bosses such as The Haunt on floors such as the Cathedral. This was later removed.
  • As of Booster Pack #5, Portals will now leave a Purple "blood" splatter when killed by the player. This effect is only for aesthetic purposes. Formerly, they left the generic Red blood splatter.
  • Lil Portals resemble Warp Zones from Super Meat Boy.
