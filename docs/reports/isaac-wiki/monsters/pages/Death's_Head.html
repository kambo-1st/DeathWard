Death's Head

 

Entity Image


Entity ID

212.0

Found In







Death's Heads are invulnerable, flying enemies that move diagonally around the room and deal contact damage. They will bounce off of Isaac, walls, and each other, generally causing chaos in a room. They immediately die when the room is cleared. They can be killed if hit with the Chaos Card, Little Horn, or Euthanasia.

Cursed Death's Heads behave the same as regular Death's Heads but have a purple aura around them. If Isaac enters the aura, he will be inflicted with the fear status effect for around two seconds, unable to fire tears for the duration of the effect.

Ultra Death Heads behave uniquely to other Death's Heads and more similarly to Dusty Death Heads, oscillating up and down in large wave-like patterns, going off-screen and reentering on the opposite side. They are summoned in a horde as a part of one of Ultra Death's attacks and are invulnerable, only being destroyed at the end of the attack.

Trivia

  • Death's Head, Flesh Death's Head, Stonies, Cross Stonies, and Eggies are the only non-boss enemies with a death animation.
  • In the game's files, the Dank Death's Head has a death animation that is impossible to see because it needs to be killed to clear a room.
  • The Death's Head is based on the Bubble from The Legend of Zelda on the NES, as they are both skulls that are usually invulnerable which maneuver by bouncing off of walls.
  • The Dusty Death's Head is based on the Medusa Head, as both fly in a wave-like pattern that is intended to be difficult to dodge.
  • In a closet room in Womb, there is a room with 1 Red Poop, 4 Blood Donation Machines, and 4 Flesh Death's Heads. When entering the room, they appear to die quickly since no other enemies in the room exist there.
  • If a Death's Head is permanently charmed by charming a Void in Chapter 3, it cannot interact with enemies or enemy tears and will simply fly around the room.
  • Prior to Booster Pack #5, if Death Heads were in the process of dying, they could still damage Isaac.
  • In Antibirth, Cursed Death's Heads were called Smokys. They could also spawn in the Chest.
  • Cursed Death's Heads resemble The Heretic.
  • Redskulls have a stray pixel on the top-right of their sprite which does appear in-game, and is animated similar to their fire.
