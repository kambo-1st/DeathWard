Boom Flies fly across the room diagonally in straight diagonal lines, dealing contact damage. Explodes upon death.

Notes

  • While Skatole is held, the Boom Fly and all of its variants will fly significantly slower and will not deal contact damage, but their other means of attacking/dealing damage will still damage Isaac as normal.
  • While Isaac has the Beelzebub transformation, all Boom Fly variants will become friendly, and will not damage Isaac by any means (including explosions).

Sick Boom Flies fly across the room in straight diagonal lines, dealing contact damage and trailing poisonous gas. Upon death, they explode into eight clouds of gas and release 6 green shots in all directions. The poison gas left behind upon death persists for much longer, but will dissipate when the room is cleared.

Tainted Boom Flies fly across the room in straight diagonal lines, dealing contact damage. Upon death, they will emit a cross-shaped glow for a brief period, after which they will explode. This explosion sends 4 lines of further explosions in the cardinal directions, which span the length of the room.

They are exclusive to the Ascent to Home.

Trivia

  • The Boom Fly is tied with the Sucker for being the monster with the most true variants ('true variants' being monsters with ID x.1, x.2, etc), both with 7.
  • Both Dragonfly types and the Sick Boom Fly first appeared in the Antibirth mod. In Antibirth, instead of having any stage hp they had 20 base hp.
    • Sick Boomflies would only create poison gas on death and would not shoot out six tears when dying.
