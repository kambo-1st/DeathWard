Spawned by Little Horn, Big Horn and Blister

Dark Balls chase Isaac, flying at a moderate pace and take increased knockback from tears. They're most often encountered when spawned by Little Horn, Big Horn and Blisters but can be sometimes encountered in some rare rooms in the Caves or Flooded Caves as well as Mausoleum rooms with Bishops. The Dark Balls Big Horn can spawn are larger and can either behave normally or have different behavior where they travel diagonally bouncing off walls.

Variations

Purple Ball

 

Entity Image


Entity ID

409.1

Found In

Spawned exclusively by Rag Mega and Cultists

Purple Balls behave similarly to regular Dark Balls, but are immune to all damage. 3 of them will always spawn orbiting Rag Mega and detach to chase Isaac as the boss's HP drops. Cultists spawn a variant of Purple Balls that are indestructible but slowly transform into vulnerable Dark Balls.

Purple Balls can be destroyed by any instant kill effects such as Little Horn or Ace of Diamonds.

Spawned exclusively by The Visage

Visage Plasma balls are a part of several of The Visage's attacks. They behave similar to the Dark Balls summoned by Big Horn, slowly floating diagonally around the room and bouncing off walls. If The Visage's mask is deactivated, one will travel towards it to revive the mask. If there are 3 alive at once they can be absorbed by The Visage's heart to heal it and cause it to perform a special attack.

Ultra Pestilence Fly Balls behave like regular Dark Balls and float towards Isaac dealing contact damage. They are, as the name suggests, spawned by Ultra Pestilence. Each time they take enough damage they spawn an Ultra Pestilence Fly. They can spawn multiple at once if they take a large amount of damage in one hit. Upon death they leave behind another Ultra Pestilence Fly.
