A Grub is a type of monster that appear infrequently in Caves and beyond. They are similar in appearance to Larry Jr. and can spawn alongside Larry Jr. or The Hollow in their respective boss rooms.

Grubs move around the room somewhat randomly until they line up with Isaac, after which they rapidly charge towards Isaac, similar to a Charger. Upon death, each of the Grub's segments will spawn a Maggot, Charger, or Spitty. Unlike Larry Jr. which requires each of its segments to be destroyed, as soon as one Grub segment is destroyed, the entire enemy will be destroyed.

Notes

  • Prior to Booster Pack #5, if a Grub spawned in the Flooded Caves, it had a high chance to spawn Drowned Chargers instead of Chargers.

Trivia

  • Because of its similar movement behavior and design to that of Larry Jr. (along with the fact that Grubs can spawn in a Larry Jr. boss room), Grubs are also known as "Larry Sr." by The Binding of Isaac community.

Bugs

Bug! Attempting to capture a Grub with Friendly Ball will only capture one segment of the Grub.
