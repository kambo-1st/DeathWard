Morningstars are enemies that float towards Isaac only when Isaac is moving. They get faster as they move, eventually reaching a point where Isaac cannot outrun them unless he has a high speed stat. When Isaac stops moving, their speed resets and they stop in place. They deal a full heart of contact damage.

They have 2 different appearances though this is purely visual as they behave the same.

Trivia

  • The name Morningstar comes from "Morning Star" which is one of the meanings of the name "Lucifer"- a common name for Satan.
  • Morningstars resemble the end of a morning star, a medieval club-like weapon.
  • Morningstars closely resemble Ferals, enemies from Edmund's game The End is Nigh. Both are sharp-toothed spiked balls that float in the air and pursue the player, attached to the ground by a chain.
  • "Morning Star" is the name of the track that plays while in Gehenna.
