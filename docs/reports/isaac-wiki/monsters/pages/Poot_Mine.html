Poot Mines are an enemy added in The Binding of Isaac: Repentance. They are encountered only in Dross.

Poot Mines float in place, producing lingering gas clouds and propelling themselves away from Isaac when they take damage non-consecutively. On death, they explode, causing all of the gas clouds they created to explode in a chain reaction.
