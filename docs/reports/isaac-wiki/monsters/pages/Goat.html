Goats are an enemy added in The Binding of Isaac: Repentance.

Goats usually spawn in large groups and perform attacks depending on the number of other Goats around. When there is only 1 Goat it will chase Isaac and charge, attempting to ram him, if he is to his either side. If there are 2 Goats, one will perform the charge attack, the other will stop when near Isaac to shoot two blood shots in a spread followed by three blood shots in a spread in succession, similar to Krampus. Only 2 Goats can actively attack at one time and all other Goats will attempt to stay away from Isaac but can switch places with an active Goat if Isaac draws near to it.

Black Goats act the same as Goats, but shoot three blood shots in a spread followed by four blood shots in a spread in succession, identical to Krampus. Additionally, they also gain the ability to shoot thin Brimstone lasers in the cardinal directions. When one Black Goat performs a Brimstone attack all other Black Goats mimic it and perform the attack even if they were passive and not one of the 2 active goats. If there is only one Black Goat, it still retains the Brimstone in addition to its ramming attack.
