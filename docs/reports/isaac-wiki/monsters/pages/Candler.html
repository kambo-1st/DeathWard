Candlers are an enemy added in The Binding of Isaac: Repentance. They fly around the room, bouncing off walls, and spawn flames beneath them.

Sometimes, they can spawn alongside The Haunt.

Trivia

  • The Candler first appeared in the Antibirth mod.
  • Candlers resemble Lil' Haunts with the face of The Haunt when it is in its second phase.
