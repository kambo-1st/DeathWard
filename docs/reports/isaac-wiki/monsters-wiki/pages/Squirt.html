The Squirt is a medium-sized poop enemy that does contact damage to Isaac by periodically dashing to him. Two Squirts spawn from Dingas when killed. It splits into two Dips when killed.

Hardies are a rocky variant of Squirts.

They charge at Isaac, and if they run into an obstacle/wall, a piece of them breaks off and rock projectiles are fired outwards. A Hardy will destroy most breakable obstacles on collision and is also able to reveal secret rooms if it hits the entrance of one. After running into rocks/walls three times, a Hardy will die.

  • First collision: 8 rock projectiles are fired out in a radius.
  • Second collision: 8 rock projectiles are fired out in a radius and a cluster of rock projectiles is launched in Isaac's direction.
  • Third collision: A cluster of small and large rock projectiles is launched in Isaac's direction. The small rock projectiles have shorter range and will spawn 2-3 Rock Spiders and the large rock projectiles each shoot out a small cross of 4 smaller rock projectiles upon landing.

In the uncommon event that it charges but doesn't hit a wall or obstacle, it will slow down like a normal Squirt without sustaining damage. Upon death, it spawns a Dip.

Trivia

  • The Hardy first appeared in the Antibirth mod where its third impact with an obstacle would cause the same effects as its first and second impact but also create rocks to fall from the ceiling.
  • Squirts are likely a reference to Zols, big Gels that split into two Gels upon death.
