A Maggot is a type of monster introduced in the vanilla content of The Binding of Isaac: Rebirth. They can be commonly found in Chapter 2. They can also be found wandering about within libraries.

As their name suggests, they're blind, white insect maggots that lack any appendages. They crawl around the room slowly and aimlessly, but will deal damage upon full contact.

Small Maggots are a type of Maggots added in The Binding of Isaac: Repentance DLC. They can be exclusively found in Corpse.

They are quite similar to regular Maggots but are variants that are very small in size. Shortly after spawning, they very slowly crawl towards Isaac. They are primarily spawned by Pustules, Level 2 Horfs , and Corpse's bosses.

Trivia

  • The Maggots first appear in the original Flash version of The Binding of Isaac.
  • Small Maggots originate from the Antibirth mod.
