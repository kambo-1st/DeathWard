Pokies are invulnerable enemies that slide around the room aimlessly in cardinal directions, dealing contact damage. They may move slightly faster towards Isaac if he gets in their sight. When the enemies or Pressure Plates are cleared from the room, the Poky stops moving and no longer does contact damage.

Variations

Slide

Entity Image


Entity ID

44.1

Found In








Chance to replace Pokies

Slides will remain stationary in their positions and have their spikes drawn out, dealing contact damage. Should Isaac get in their sight in a cardinal direction, Slides will move towards Isaac's direction, then move back to their previous position after hitting a wall or obstacle. They have a short cooldown after charging towards Isaac before being able to move again. They will not stop moving even after enemies in the room are cleared. They can, however, be deactivated by Pressure Plates and retract their spikes as a result, meaning that they'll stop dealing contact damage and also stop moving towards Isaac.

Wall Hugger

Entity ID

Found In









Wall Huggers move around walls and obstacles endlessly. A couple of seconds after Isaac enters a room with them, their spikes will be drawn out, dealing contact damage. They cannot be shut down, even if enemies are cleared from the room. They can, however, be deactivated by Pressure Plates, retracting its spikes, meaning that it'll no longer move and do contact damage. Their paths can also be stopped should a Fire Place or static enemy be in their way.

Grudge

(Mausoleum and Gehenna only)

Grudges are exclusive to Mausoleum and Gehenna and are characterized by the eye in the center and two spikes on each side instead of three. Very briefly after Isaac enters a room, their spikes will be drawn out. They remain stationary until there's an uninterrupted path between it and Isaac in a cardinal direction, at which point it will quickly slide towards him, dealing a full heart of damage if it hits. They will not return to their original position after moving. They also have unique interactions with other stage elements.

  • If it runs into an enemy, it will be pushed along with it, instantly killing it if they're caught between the Grudge and a wall/obstacle.
    • Even bosses are prone to this instant kill effect. However, the only method to witness this happening is to get a TMTRAINER effect with summoning friendly Grudges.
  • If it runs over a button, it will be pressed. Many rooms use this to create puzzles where Isaac has to manipulate the Grudge into pressing buttons.
  • If it runs into a Bomb, it will be pushed along with it, causing it to instantly explode if it gets caught between the Grudge and a wall/obstacle.

Notes

  • A Poky can appear in a Loki boss fight.
  • These monsters can be destroyed using Chaos Card, Little Horn and Euthanasia. However, Grudges will re-spawn upon re-entering the room.
  • Two Pokies can appear in a Super Pride miniboss fight.
  • In Greed mode, if a Slide is spawned and all waves are cleared, the Slide will still try to harm Isaac. Leaving and re-entering the room will make the Slide disappear.
  • All Pokies except for Grudges will retract their spikes if Isaac holds Flat File.

Trivia

  • The three original variants are similar to Blade Traps, hazards found in The Legend of Zelda, with each trap imitating the different behaviors of them between games.
    • Pokies imitate the Blade Traps from Phantom Hourglass and Spirit Tracks, being traps that move freely around the room.
    • Slides imitate the traditional Blade Traps from the original The Legend of Zelda, charging towards Isaac whenever he is in their line of detection.
    • Wall Huggers imitate the Blade Traps from Twilight Princess, moving around the room's walls and obstacles.
  • Grudges are very similar to Crush Traps from Spelunky, having a similar design with an eye in the center, the same movement pattern, and the same quirk of instantly killing enemies it traps between itself and a wall or obstacle.
  • Isaac has a unique death animation if he dies to a Grudge. He bursts into gibs like any other enemy, and his body is invisible (aside from his ghost arising from it).
    • This is a reference to the death sequence caused by crush traps, from the game Spelunky, which behave exactly like a Grudge.
  • Grudge's eyes will stare at and follow Isaac as he moves.
