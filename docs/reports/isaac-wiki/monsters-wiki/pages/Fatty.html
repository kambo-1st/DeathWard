Fatties walk slowly toward Isaac, occasionally stopping to fart, which pushes Isaac away without dealing damage if he is too close. This will also push away adjacent enemies to a lesser degree.

Peeping Fatties slowly walk towards Isaac and occasionally fart out 6 blood shots in all directions. They are unique in that they have two indestructible Peeping Fatty Eyes that float around them that are out of their sockets and attached by muscles.

Each eye generally prioritizes its focus on Isaac, but will occasionally become interested in something else, which could be an enemy, obstacle, or a collectible. The Peeping Fatty will sometimes begin pursuing whatever one or both of his eyes have become fascinated with, but will also prioritize chasing Isaac if he is in view.

If both eyes are destroyed (using Euthanasia, for example), the Fatty will become blind and wander around randomly.

Shadies follow Isaac at a faster pace than regular Fatties and have two attacks which they perform:

  • They can shoot a large black and red projectile towards Isaac which releases accelerating shots perpendicular to itself while traveling. Upon colliding with a wall, the large projectile creates a ring of shots that spread outward.
  • They can jump in place, creating lines of explosions in the cardinal directions upon landing which span the length of the room.

Notes

  • Shadies are one of the few enemies which utilize Damage Scaling, making their unimpressive HP total misleading. Their armor is quite high and allows them to take massive amounts of punishment before being taken down, comparable even to some bosses.

Vis Fatties spawn with a Fetal Demon attached to their slit abdomens. The Fatty will slowly walk towards the player while the Fetal Demon occasionally lunges towards Isaac if he gets too close. As it is tethered, the Demon has a limited range of movement. Occasionally, the Vis Fatty will fart, which briefly pushes the Fetal Demon away, giving it a substantial increase in range, as well as creating puddles of damaging Red Creep around the Fatty, the Demon, and the cord that attaches them.

If the Fatty is killed first, the Demon will be broken free and continuously chase down the player similar to Gemini Suture. If the Demon is killed first, the Vis Fatty unleashes a Brimstone laser in the closest diagonal direction to Isaac. It will then fly backwards around the room diagonally, bumping off of walls and tall blocks, all while continuously shooting Brimstone behind it.

Gutted Fatties lumber around the room aimlessly and occasionally shoot out a pile of Festering Guts downwards leaving a trail of Red Creep. They can spawn a maximum of 3.

Every time a third of the Gutted Fatty's health is depleted, it spawns a Gutted Fatty Eye. These eyes normally float around the Gutted Fatty they emerged from and block shots. Whenever Isaac walks too close to one of these eyes, they will abandon their position and begin slowly chasing him instead. The eyes will return to defending the Gutted Fatty if Isaac walks far enough away from them. The eyes only die when the Gutted Fatty dies.

Upon death, the Gutted Fatty explodes into a massive shower of blood projectiles, similar to that of Bubbles.

Gutted Fatty Eye

   

Entity Image


Entity ID

831.10

Found In

Spawned in pairs by Gutted Fatties.

Gutted Fatty Eyes spawn in pairs, but do not bounce around the room erratically like similar enemies. They are spawned by a Gutted Fatty as it takes damage, one at 66% HP and a second at 33% HP. When spawned, they will hover around their correlating Gutted Fatty blocking tears. They will be destroyed once their correlating Gutted Fatty dies.

Whenever Isaac walks too close to one of these eyes, they will abandon their position and begin slowly chasing him instead, moving in wave-like patterns. If both eyes are chasing Isaac, they will maintain a distance between the two as they move in order to cover more area. They will return to defending the Gutted Fatty if Isaac walks far enough away from them.

Stoney

Entity Image


Entity ID

302.0

Found In







Chance to replace Pale Fatties

Unlock Method

Defeat The Lamb

Stoneys walk towards Isaac while blocking his shots. They stop and catch their breath after chasing Isaac for a few seconds. They do not do contact damage but will often push Isaac around. They are invincible, but die when the room is cleared, similar to the Death's Head enemies. Stoneys are unlocked by defeating The Lamb for the first time.

Notes

  • If Stoneys aren't unlocked yet, they will be replaced by Fatties in Normal mode, or Conjoined Fatties in Hard Mode.
  • Spectral tears and piercing tears will pierce through Stoneys.
  • If they are poisoned with The Virus, upon clearing the room, they have a chance to drop a Black Heart despite not taking damage.
  • Stoneys are immune to all status effects.
  • Stoneys can be killed with Chaos Card, Euthanasia, and Little Horn.
  • Stoneys were nerfed in update v1.06.0188 by making them pause in exhaustion after following the player. Before that, Stoneys would continually chase the player.

Entity ID

302.10

A Cross Stoney is a type of Stoney added in Booster Pack #5. These new enemies act like a hybridization of a Stoney and a Cross Stone Shooter; they constantly chase the player like a Stoney but will shoot 4 shots outwards vertically and diagonally like a Cross Grimace.

Cross Stoneys only appear in challenge #32 (Aprils Fool) as replacements to regular Stoneys.

Trivia

  • Death's Head, Flesh Death's Head, Stonies, Cross Stonies, and Eggies are the only non-boss enemies with a death animation.
  • Conjoined Fatties and Bloaties are one of five enemies (four without Repentance, three without Afterbirth+) in the game that use Creep as projectiles, the others are Ultra Pride, Meatballs and The Thing.
  • Blubbers appear to be headless Pale Fatties or, more accurately, headless Fat Sacks.
    • Similarly, Half Sacks appear to be the lower portions of Pale Fatties or, more accurately, the lower portions of Fat Sacks.
  • Conjoined Fatties and Blue Conjoined Fatties suffer the condition of polycephaly, having more than one head.
  • Conjoined Fatties and Blue Conjoined Fatties make the same high-pitched growling noises as Gapers instead of the lower-pitched ones used by other Fatties.
  • Stoneys make the sounds of a pitched-down Gaper.
    • Stoneys make the sounds of a pitched-down Fatty.
  • Antibirth developer Kilburn first revealed Cross Stoneys on the 1st of April for Booster Pack #5.[1]
    • It was revealed on the 1st of April, and Cross Stoneys are exclusive to the Aprils Fool Challenge.
  • Bubbles, Quakies, and Big Bonies originate from the Antibirth mod.
    • In Antibirth, Quakies have a different face.
    • The Big Bony's bone attack is also different from Antibirth, where the bone was instead a lobbed projectile that created shockwaves in the place of its impact. Big Bonies also used to spawn in Mausoleum.
  • Vis Fatties look very similar to Exorcists.
  • Bloaty's name and design is somewhat a reminiscent to the human corpse's bloating stage, which causes the corpse to swell up and bear swollen lips and eyeballs due to gas.
  • Shadies are inspired by the Shadow Ninja enemy from the Shadow Warrior game series.[2]
