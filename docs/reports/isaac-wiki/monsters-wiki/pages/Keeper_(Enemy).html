This article is about the monster. For the playable character, see Keeper.

Keepers (also known as "Greed Heads") are bodiless Hangers, commonly found in Catacombs and the Dank Depths. They can also be spawned by Super Greed and Ultra Greed, or found alongside Hangers. They move like Hoppers and occasionally stop to shoot two projectiles in a V shape towards Isaac. If hit by these projectiles, Isaac will drop coins.
