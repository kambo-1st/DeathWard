Dukie is an enemy added in The Binding of Isaac: Afterbirth. It floats diagonally around the room, occasionally spitting out 1-2 Dart Flies. Dukies do not attack in any other way but can still do contact damage.

Notes

  • If there are 6 Dart Flies spawned, Dukies will stop spawning Dart Flies.

  • The Duke of Flies is the boss version of these enemies.
