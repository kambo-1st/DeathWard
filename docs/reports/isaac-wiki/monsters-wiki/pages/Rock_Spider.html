Rock Spiders are small spider-legged stones and they were added to The Binding of Isaac: Repentance. They behave very closely to Spiders, but they are exclusive to Mines and Ashpit and skitter at a slower speed

Upon death, they turn into a normal Spider.

Tinted Rock Spiders are similar to Rock Spiders, however they have nearly twice as much health and move much faster. When killed, instead of transforming into a regular Spider, they drop 1 Soul Heart upon death. They are characterized by the "X" on their rock, which is similar to Tinted Rocks.

They do not spawn naturally; there is a chance that 1-2 will spawn upon destruction of a Tinted Rock while in the Mines or Ashpit.

Coal Spiders behave very closely to Rock Spiders, but they skitter at a faster speed due to being on fire.

Upon death, it leaves a hot rock on the ground that despawns after several seconds. They can be shot to make them despawn faster.

Rock Spiders coming into contact with a Fire Place or fire spawned by items, such as Red Candle or Bird's Eye, turn into Coal Spiders.

Trivia

  • The Rock Spider and all its variants originate from the Antibirth mod.
