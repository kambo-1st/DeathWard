Babies slowly float towards Isaac, occasionally shooting a single projectile towards him. They can also occasionally teleport to anywhere in the room.

Wrinkly Babies slowly move towards Isaac similar to regular Babies, but while approaching him, they may dash towards him at high speed until they hit either a wall or Isaac and then subsequently fire three projectiles in his direction once they stop dashing. If Isaac approaches them after they've performed their previous attack, they will move back from Isaac and fire three projectiles at him without dashing for as long as Isaac continues to follow them, although they will resume their chase and dashing attack if Isaac then moves far from them. They are unable to teleport.

Baby Begottens float toward Isaac, attempting to avoid Isaac's shots as it approaches. After getting close to him, it lines itself up with him in the closest cardinal direction and fires a thin short-range Brimstone laser at him, propelling itself in the opposite direction.

Despite their namesake and visual similarities to Begottens they have no similarities in behavior.
