Brains slide around the room erratically in the cardinal directions, leaving a trail of Red Creep, they deal a whole heart of contact damage. When Isaac is within line of sight they will charge in his direction until they collide with an obstacle or another enemy similar to Knights.

Pons hop around erratically leaving Red Creep wherever they land. If close to Isaac then they will hop towards Isaac chasing him similarly to Hoppers.

They can spawn with a paired Brainless Knight in which killing them is required to kill their corresponding Brainless Knight. A Pon and a Brainless Knight pair will always spawn when Loose Knight is killed.


Notes

  • If Poison Minds aren't unlocked yet, they are replaced by Brains.

Trivia

  • The Pon first appeared in the Antibirth mod where it could also appear in Depths.
  • The name of Pon is likely in reference to the Pons region of the brain, a part of the brainstem associated with respiration and other autonomic functions.
