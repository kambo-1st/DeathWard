Gyros were introduced in The Binding of Isaac: Repentance. They look like a piece of meat with a bone right through the middle.

They move themselves by rolling — similar to how a wheel moves. When Isaac is in their line of sight, they will charge at him quickly and can rotate to continue pursuing him for a short time. Afterward, the Gyro will briefly quit chasing Isaac and slowly roll around as if in exhaustion before resuming its normal behavior. Gyros will convert into Grilled Gyros, if in contact with fire.
