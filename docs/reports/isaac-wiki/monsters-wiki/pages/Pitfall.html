Pitfall

Entity Image


Entity ID

291.0

Found In

Only spawned by Little Horn.

Pitfalls are invulnerable and stationary enemies which Isaac can walk over. When Isaac walks over a pitfall, he falls in, takes damage, and pops back out after a short delay. Pitfalls disappear after falling into them. They are only spawned by several of Little Horn's attacks.

Notes

  • Flight makes Isaac immune to pitfalls.

Variations

Suction Pitfall

 

Entity Image


Entity ID

291.1

Found In




Suction Pitfalls act as regular pitfalls, but suck Isaac in like a Gaping Maw. They are typically found in rooms with Pressure Plates, which when pressed will make any Suction Pitfalls in the room disappear.

Teleport Pitfall

 

Entity Image


Entity ID

291.2

Found In

Only spawned by Little Horn.


Teleport Pitfalls act as regular pitfalls, but pop Isaac out at a random location after damaging him. Little Horn spawns these and pushes them outward in some of his attacks.

Bugs

Bug! Falling into a Pitfall while a Black Hole is active will result in Isaac becoming headless, immobile and invulnerable, softlocking the run until it is quit and resumed.
Bug! If Isaac walks over a Teleport Pitfall while Mega Mush is active a visual bug will occur where the pitfall is destroyed and can no longer harm Isaac however its sprites still remain in the room. When Isaac reenters the room or exits and continues the run, the teleport pitfall works normally again.
