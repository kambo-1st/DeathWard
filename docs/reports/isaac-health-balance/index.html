Regular-enemy HP: source values, adaptations and testing

2026-11-01T11:11:00.914034+00:00 · 155 source profiles and 32 resolutions · Wiki comparison

Repentance 1.7.9b is the baseline. All 155 reviewed regular identities have source base HP and stage HP matching preserved XML metadata. Twelve explicit adaptation factors preserve existing starting balance. The multiplier remains 1; no across-the-board HP reduction has been applied.

Future HP adjustments

Edit assets/mechanics/regular_enemy_health.mechanics:

regular_health_multiplier 1

For 25% less HP, use regular_health_multiplier .75. For half HP, use .5. Range: .05–4. The setting is read with the other mechanics files; restart the game and begin a new expedition to use it. Existing live snapshots retain their previous tuning.

Player defaults stay at 100 HP / 24 damage. Enemy attack damage and boss body HP are separate. Regular minions use their own regular HP settings.

Calculation

HP = (source base HP + source stage HP × stage factor)
     × 3.2 conversion × explicit adaptation × regular health multiplier

stage factor = min(source stage, 4) + 0.8 × clamp(source stage − 5, 0, 5)

The expedition floor maps to a source stage through source_stages; they are not interchangeable. Champion HP and offspring fractions apply once after this calculation. Supported HP regeneration, growth floors and source-health thresholds use the same balance adjustment. Attack damage does not inherit the HP multiplier.

Example: normal Charger = 20 × 3.2 = 64 HP. At .75 it has 48 HP; its Red champion has 48 × 2.6 = 124.8 HP. Neutral 24-damage shots take 2 and 6 hits respectively. Armor and phases can change actual combat results.

Validation and limits

HP tests check all 155 identities across 13 source stages at half HP, actual spawning, champions, unchanged player defaults/damage, regeneration floors, growth and proportional offspring/overkill. Shared controller regressions are stored alongside this report. No enemy is promoted to fully accepted on this basis.

  • Initial/max HP normalization does not certify each enemy AI or special item interaction.
  • Boss body HP and unreviewed actors are excluded; regular minions remain in scope.
  • Neutral shot counts exclude armor, hit regions, phases, healing and weapon modifiers.
  • Legacy starting HP is preserved. Holy Mulligan and Cloggy now grow with source stage instead of staying flat.
