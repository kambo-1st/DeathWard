## Current selected health implementation

Players start with three full red hearts (six half-heart units), with separate soul/black protection, eternal and golden overlays, ordered bone containers, rotten hearts and broken capacity. Damage, medicine, container upgrades and current health trades now use this typed model. Ordinary authored 10-unit contacts remove one half-heart unit; the representative six-loss survival case and ordinary native hurt counter are covered by integration tests. Universal dodge keeps its speed boost and immunity. Player attack damage remains 24, enemy HP and its regular-health multiplier are unchanged.

Health, inventory and owned blue flies persist between expedition floors. Eternal conversion adds/fills one red container without healing other injuries. Ordinary death checks all living health types, and an explicitly granted 1up health dependency provides one scoped revival. Typed world pickups and a mixed-heart HUD are enabled; placement support does not establish natural availability.

See [H02–H18 implementation, evidence and unfinished work](reports/isaac-first-floor-parity/h02-h18-health/README.md). This separates re-executed native arithmetic from enabled gameplay and full acceptance. Native health fixtures cover 293 transitions; hurt fixtures cover 24 counter cases and 193 decrement states. Midas, blue-fly and rotten-refill captures are checked independently. The health display has been inspected, and eight focused suites and four Game/input modes pass. The broader pass has 106 passing suites after targeted reruns; ten other failures reproduce on the untouched baseline and are documented in the health report.

**Still unfinished:** full damage-entry flags/source-specific exceptions; special black/Midas/blue-fly target interactions; native mixed prices, pickup survival grace, wider health/revival items and character transformations; source pickup physics and natural drop rules; live native comparison and user visual acceptance. In particular H11/H14/H16/H17 are not claims that every native flag or item exception is implemented. Current authored health services remain documented adaptations. P04/P09 and the wider stat, weapon, enemy, item, room and reward differences remain unselected.
