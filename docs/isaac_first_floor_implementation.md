#### Recommendation basis: Isaac combat balance comes first

Reviewed 2026-11-08T10:34:37+00:00 against the health commit `3ad25797`, current firing/movement/build code, the original audit and the pinned native research below. These are recommendations, **not implementation authorization**. No new S gameplay implementation is selected. S06 now records the user's retained eight-direction firing/mouse-keyboard control exception; S13 retains universal dodge.

The user clarified that **porting Isaac combat balance is crucial: bullet range, enemy HP, attack speed and their combined effect must match the intended source experience**. Prioritize observable combat outcomes over matching displayed numbers or preserving an unintended DeathWard balance difference. Graphics, universal dodge, and the current eight-direction firing mechanism with existing mouse/keyboard controls are the accepted exceptions. **Do not replace current firing with Isaac's four-direction restriction or change its input mapping.** Balance range, damage, enemy HP and attack speed around these retained controls; exact Isaac difficulty cannot be inferred from matching numbers alone. No other weapon or item exception is approved by this review.

**Damage, enemy HP and cadence need one coherent unit plan.** Keep source base HP, source-stage HP, DeathWard conversion/adaptation and the independent regular-health multiplier distinct. Map the expedition floor to its source stage. Audit each enemy conversion; do not assume every actor uses the same factor or change `regular_health_multiplier` to conceal a mismatch.

Attack speed must be measured from actual emissions. The archived ordinary native firing evidence supports `30/(10+1)` ≈ 2.727 shots/s; the original DeathWard probe measured 3.333 shots/s. Native source-unit DPS of about 9.545 and DeathWard raw DPS of 80 are not directly comparable until HP/damage units are reconciled. Neither continuous DPS nor `HP/DPS` alone proves discrete time to kill: measure first-shot latency, travel time, required hits, missed/blocked shots and vulnerable windows as well.

Range must likewise be measured in combat space. Displayed Range 6.5 is not a 6.5-world-unit cutoff; the native flight evidence distinguishes nominal cached range from actual ground expiry and traveled distance. DeathWard's original `26 × 3.2 = 83.2` unobstructed lifetime reach is a current-policy calculation, not source parity. Shot speed, logical fall, inherited motion, player/enemy hulls and walls must be calibrated together. Keep 3D rendering/terrain height distinct from the logical targeting rules.

- An interactive baseline and candidate, each with a single launch command or a clearly labelled A/B switch, using the same seed/room, camera, zoom and spawn. These review demos are a deliverable of future selected work; this report does not claim they have already been built.
- A short list of exactly what changed and the relevant measurements: crossing time/speed for movement; travel/reach for projectiles; intervals for firing; hits/kill times for damage/HP. Present known uncertainty alongside the measurements.
- Completed relevant automated checks, a baseline code/config identifier, and a straightforward reversible way to return to the baseline. Keep local comparison binaries out of Git.
- A persisted human result: **pending**, **accepted**, or **needs revision**, tied to that exact candidate. Passing tests, a screenshot, elapsed time or silence does not count as human acceptance. If the candidate needs revision, fix that stage and repeat its check before proceeding.

**Reopen earlier checks when their inputs change.** A later coordinate-scale, collider, frame-scheduling or movement change invalidates earlier movement approval; repeat the movement A/B before continuing. This catches regressions while the change is small, rather than relying on one final expedition playtest.

## Per-floor shop and upgrade counts and positions

The earlier audit covered shops,treasure rewards and room layouts generally. **It did not verify the proper source counts per floor or their placement constraints.** R16–R23 now track these as individually selectable differences/verification requirements. Nothing has been selected or implemented.

“Upgrades” must distinguish Power/treasure rooms from actual collectible rewards: room count,number of offers,pedestal count,number of choices and obtainable items are different quantities. Include boss rewards and shop collectibles when calculating total opportunities.

Current committed DeathWard source establishes:

- Non-tutorial floors assign one Shop room. Shop candidates are eligible ordinary room IDs>=4; smaller floors reserve their site before allocating keys and quiet rooms.
- A floor with fewer than12 rooms gets one Power room; a floor with12–15 rooms selects one or two. Cache placement uses authored branch/junction and shallow-floor preferences. These are not counts of all upgrades obtainable on the floor.
- The Lost Survey has no Shop or Power rooms. Its five-room tutorial is a separate exception.

These observations come from [room-graph generation](../src/world/RoomGraph.cpp) and [room assignment](../src/world/Dungeon.cpp); they are not verified Isaac counts. **The exact Repentance1.7.9b shop/treasure counts,exceptions,placement rules and in-room item positions remain unverified.** No guessed “one of everything on every floor” rule is recorded as fact.

The eventual comparison must record,for each seed and expedition floor,its mapped source floor/stage; shop and treasure-room counts; boss and other collectible reward opportunities; room IDs and graph coordinates; links and distance from the entrance; relative positions; and stock/pedestal positions inside each room. Capture the generation rules as well as example coordinates. Do not use one fixed coordinate as a substitute for seeded placement,and do not generalize Basement-I findings to every later floor.
